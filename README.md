# 🛡️ CashGuard AI

### AI-Powered Financial Intelligence & Cash Flow Management Platform

CashGuard AI is a modern fintech platform designed to help freelancers, startups, SMEs, and service-based businesses manage their finances, predict cash flow, identify payment risks, and make smarter financial decisions.

The platform combines financial analytics, AI-powered predictions, fraud detection, payment anomaly detection, and an AI finance copilot into a single dashboard.
